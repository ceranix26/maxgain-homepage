# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are B2B electronics professionals: purchasing and sourcing teams, engineers, and R&D teams looking to find and evaluate electronic components. They need to search by manufacturer part number, check supply and stock availability, review specifications and possible alternatives, access technical resources, and contact MAXGAIN for quotations or sourcing support. Existing customers and manufacturer or supplier partners are secondary users who check product coverage, resources, and contact information.

## Product Purpose

MAXGAIN SYSTEMS' bilingual website helps B2B customers move from a known part number or component requirement to useful product and sourcing information, then to a quotation, lead-time, or technical inquiry. Success means finding relevant information quickly and knowing how to request the next step without treating the site as a consumer store.

## Positioning

MAXGAIN is a practical B2B electronic component sourcing and distribution partner, not an online retail marketplace. Its value is helping customers move efficiently from a part number or component need to actionable sourcing information, including support for hard-to-find, obsolete, and lead-time-sensitive components.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse manufacturer and product-category coverage, search by part number, review product availability and technical resources, and contact MAXGAIN for quotations, sourcing, lead-time, or technical inquiries. The website serves Korean- and English-speaking visitors.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Part Number is the primary product search key and must remain easy to find and use.
- Product discovery includes manufacturer and category browsing, product specifications, and suitable or alternative part information where validated.
- Public inventory should communicate availability or stock-level bands, not exact internal quantities. Confirm band definitions and freshness expectations before changing how stock status is presented.
- Product, manufacturer, specification, catalog, datasheet, and technical-resource information must come from validated manufacturer or otherwise reliable sources. Do not invent, infer, or silently correct it.
- Represent only manufacturers and product categories MAXGAIN actually handles. Do not add or remove them, or change business claims, without explicit approval.
- Do not expose purchasing cost, sales pricing, customer or supplier-sensitive information, ERP data, or other internal business data.
- This is not a consumer e-commerce store. Do not add carts, checkout, consumer pricing, discounts, or similar retail functionality without explicit approval.
- Preserve Korean/English language support and consistent language behavior across navigation and page content.
- Preserve existing product-search, inventory-data, resource-data, and shared-JavaScript behavior when making design changes.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the MAXGAIN SYSTEMS name and preserve Korean/English support.
- Maintain a professional, technically credible B2B voice focused on clarity and efficient sourcing rather than marketing-heavy claims.
- Do not repeat company-history claims, including a founding year, unless verified against an authoritative source.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing site pages document MAXGAIN SYSTEMS, manufacturer coverage, product categories, product search, contact paths, and technical resources.
- `data/inventory-status.json` provides inventory-status data for the public product-search experience; do not expose exact internal quantities or source-system details.
- Manufacturer and resource records are maintained under `manufacturers/` and `data/resources/`.
- The existing logo is at `assets/images/maxgain-logo.png`.
- The site contains historical/company claims, but their authoritative verification is not established here. Do not treat them as verified proof or extend them without confirmation.
- Do not fabricate customer names, testimonials, performance benchmarks, pricing, or other proof.

## Product Principles

1. Make part-number discovery fast and dependable.
2. Show useful availability and technical information without disclosing internal business data.
3. Keep sourcing and inquiry actions close to product discovery.
4. Earn trust through accurate, validated information rather than unsupported claims.
5. Serve professional B2B workflows, not consumer shopping behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific accessibility standard or user accommodation has been confirmed. Preserve accessible interactions, clear information hierarchy, responsive behavior, and consistent Korean/English language support.
