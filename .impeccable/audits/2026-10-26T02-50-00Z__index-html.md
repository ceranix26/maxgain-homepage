## Detailed Findings

### P2 — Hero autoplay eventually downloads the full video

- **Location:** [index.html](../../index.html), hero video element and playback initialization
- **Category:** Performance
- **Impact:** `preload="none"` avoids an immediate media request, but idle-scheduled autoplay still requests and plays the approximately **1.48 MB** video under standard motion settings. This can consume data on metered or constrained connections without an explicit playback action.
- **Recommendation:** If unsolicited data use is a concern, keep the poster and wait for a user-initiated play action. If autoplay is essential to the hero, consider a smaller or more efficient encode.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable optimize`
