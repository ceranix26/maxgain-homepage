# Homepage critique — `index.html`

**Method:** Two isolated assessments: a design review and a detector/browser evidence pass. I reviewed the rendered desktop page; the second assessment also inspected mobile and confirmed the detector overlay ran. The critique CLI subcommand is not exposed in this project's launcher, so the review followed the critique playbook directly.

**Overall impression:** The homepage looks polished and communicates sourcing expertise, but it introduces the company before helping a known-part-number visitor act. Its clearest opportunity is making that first action immediate and grounding trust claims in verified evidence.

## Design-specificity verdict

**Moderately product-specific in content; visually conventional.** The copy names real B2B sourcing needs—hard-to-find and obsolete parts, lead-time review, alternatives—and manufacturer links add MAXGAIN-specific evidence. But the full-bleed logistics video, capability statements, company-history block, logo marquee, and orange quote band follow a familiar supplier-landing-page pattern. The most product-specific action, part-number search, is not the hero's focus.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

**Moderate; 2 checklist failures.** The hero groups three capabilities and is legible, but does not establish one clear first action. Desktop shows five navigation destinations plus two language controls—seven choices at the header decision point. On mobile, Product Search requires opening the menu.

The animated hero creates energy, then the page moves into company history and partner logos without giving a part-number visitor an immediate next step. The closing quote/search CTA restores direction, but only after scrolling. At this trust-sensitive moment, the history claim needs verification rather than serving as proof.

## What's working

- Copy is grounded in professional sourcing needs rather than consumer-shopping language.
- The hero has clear typographic hierarchy and strong text contrast.
- The closing CTA offers understandable routes to request a quote or search products.

## Priority issues

1. **[P1] Part-number search is not the first-screen action.** A known-part visitor must find Product Search in navigation—or open the mobile menu—and the dedicated CTA is much farther down. **Fix:** Add a direct part-number search entry/action to the hero, with quote request secondary. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape index.html`.
2. **[P1] Unverified company-history claims are used as trust proof.** The page says "30 years" and "Since 1992"; the project context says these have not been authoritatively verified. **Fix:** Verify and approve them or use an approved date-free capability statement; do not replace factual copy without approval. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify index.html`.
3. **[P2] The mobile menu's accessible label stays Korean in English mode.** English-speaking screen-reader users hear `메뉴 열기` while navigation is in English. **Fix:** Localize the accessible name and synchronize it with the selected language and open/closed state. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify index.html`.
4. **[P2] The manufacturer marquee has no touch-friendly pause.** CSS pauses on hover, unavailable to touch users; focusing a logo does not pause it. Reduced-motion preference does disable animation. **Fix:** Pause on keyboard focus and touch, or use a static/user-controlled strip. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable quieter index.html`.
